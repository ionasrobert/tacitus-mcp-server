Cancellation & Refunds

Version 1.0 · in force from 14 August 2026 · part of the Terms of Service

Cancel whenever you like, from Stripe, without asking us. Within the first 14 days you get your money back in full, no reason required.

How to cancel

  1. Open the subscription portal and enter the email address you used at checkout. Stripe sends you a sign-in link; from there you can cancel, change the payment card, switch between monthly and yearly, and download your invoices.
  2. Or write to office@docsprinting.ro from that same email address and we will cancel it for you.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Pro stays active for the period you have already paid for; there is no partial refund for the remainder of a period, except under the withdrawal right below.

The 14-day withdrawal right

If you are a consumer in the European Union, you may withdraw from the contract within 14 days without giving any reason (Directive 2011/83/EU; in Romania, OUG 34/2014). In practice we apply it generously:

  • First payment: a full refund if you ask within 14 days of subscribing.
  • Annual renewals: a full refund if you ask within 14 days of the renewal charge — we would rather refund a renewal you did not want than keep it.
  • Send the request to office@docsprinting.ro with the email address that paid, or use the portal and tell us. No form is required; a plain sentence is enough.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method, normally within 5–10 working days once Stripe has processed them. Because the service starts immediately at your request, we may retain a proportionate amount for the days used before withdrawal, but as a matter of practice we do not.

What happens to your vault when Pro ends

Nothing is deleted, and nothing surprising happens:

  • Your notes are plain Markdown files on your own devices. They are untouched by anything that happens to a subscription.
  • The vault returns to the free 100 MB quota on the hosted relay. Its existing encrypted log stays where it is.
  • If that log is larger than the free quota, the relay stops accepting new updates for it and says so. Your client's answer is to compact — it seals the current state into a fresh snapshot and the relay drops everything beneath it, which almost always brings a vault back under the free quota. Compaction is always permitted, even over quota, precisely because it is the cure.
  • If it still does not fit, you can shrink the vault, keep syncing a smaller one, run your own relay (no quotas at all), or resubscribe. In no case do we delete your log without telling you.

If something goes wrong

Write to office@docsprinting.ro. If we cannot settle it between us, consumers in Romania may turn to the National Authority for Consumer Protection (ANPC), its alternative dispute resolution service (SAL), or the European Commission's online dispute resolution platform (SOL/ODR).

Cancelling is genuinely self-serve: the portal above is Stripe's own, it authenticates you by emailing a sign-in link, and it cancels at the end of the period you already paid for.
