1. The Tacitus software (local use)

Run on its own, Tacitus reads and writes files in a folder you choose. It makes no network requests of any kind, collects no usage statistics, and contains no telemetry or crash reporting. If you never enable sync, we never learn that you exist.

2. The hosted relay (sync.tacitus.md)

When you enable sync against our relay, we process:

  • The vault id — 32 hexadecimal characters derived one-way from your vault code on your device. It identifies the log; it reveals nothing about you or your notes and cannot be reversed into the vault code.
  • Encrypted update blobs — your notes, sealed with a key derived on your devices. We cannot decrypt them. Note titles, paths, tags and memory contents are all inside the ciphertext.
  • Technical metadata we cannot avoid: the size and timestamp of each update, the sequence number, and how many devices are connected to a vault. This is what makes a log a log.
  • Connection data — your IP address appears in the web-server access logs in front of the relay, as it does for any website. These logs are kept for a short period (currently up to 14 days) and then deleted.

We do not profile, sell, share, or mine any of this. We do not have a mechanism to associate a vault id with a person unless that vault has a Pro subscription, in which case Stripe holds the billing identity — see section 4.

A relay operator can see that a vault exists, roughly how large it is, and when it is active. A relay operator can withhold data. A relay operator can never read, forge, or splice your content. The full threat model is published in docs/SYNC.md.

3. Legal bases (GDPR art. 6)

  • Performance of a contract — storing and forwarding your encrypted log is the service you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests — short-lived access logs, to keep the service available and to investigate abuse.
  • Legal obligation — accounting and tax records for paid subscriptions.

4. Processors we use

  • Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. (payments) — when you subscribe to Pro, Stripe collects your email, payment details and any tax identifiers, and stores them under its own privacy policy. We receive from Stripe only the subscription status and the vault id we sent it. We never receive card numbers.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH — the server that runs the relay, in a German data centre.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — DNS and reverse proxy in front of the relay. It sees connection metadata and the same ciphertext we do.

We use no analytics provider, no advertising network, and no third-party fonts or scripts on this website. This site sets no cookies of its own; our proxy may set strictly necessary security cookies.

5. Retention

  • Encrypted log: kept while the vault is in use. Your client compacts the log by replacing old entries with a sealed snapshot; the replaced data is deleted from our disks at that point. A vault with no activity for 12 months may be deleted in full.
  • Access logs: up to 14 days.
  • Billing records: as long as Romanian accounting law requires (currently 10 years), held by us and by Stripe.

6. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection under the GDPR. Two practical notes, both consequences of the design rather than evasions:

  • Your notes are already in your possession as plain files — portability is the default state, not a request you have to make.
  • Because we hold no identity for a vault, we cannot verify that a vault id belongs to you. We therefore act on requests concerning a vault only from someone who can demonstrate control of it (for example, from the email that paid for its subscription, or by performing an action on the vault we can observe).

To delete a vault's stored log, write to office@docsprinting.ro with the vault id. Requests are answered within 30 days. You may also complain to the Romanian supervisory authority, ANSPDCP, or to the authority where you live.

7. International transfers

The relay runs on servers in the European Union. Stripe and Cloudflare may process data outside the EU under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

8. Changes

Any change is published here with a new version number and date; material changes are announced on the site before they take effect.

That retention is enforced, not merely intended: the web server rotates its logs daily and keeps 13 archives — the current day plus thirteen. Nothing older than 14 days survives.
