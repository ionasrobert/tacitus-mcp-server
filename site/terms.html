1. What the service is

Tacitus Sync stores an append-only log of end-to-end encrypted updates for a vault and forwards them to your other devices. Encryption and decryption happen on your devices. We hold ciphertext and an opaque vault identifier; we cannot read, search, or reconstruct your notes, and no support request can change that.

  • Free tier: 100 MB of encrypted log per vault, all features enabled.
  • Pro: 1 GB of encrypted log per vault, at €5 per month or €48 per year, per vault. Prices are in EUR and include VAT where it applies; the exact amount charged, including any tax, is shown at checkout.

2. No accounts — the vault id is the whole relationship

There is no sign-up, no password, and no profile. A vault is identified by a 32-character hexadecimal vault id derived one-way from your vault code on your own device. Consequently:

  • Anyone who has your vault code can read that vault. Keep it as you would keep a private key. We never receive it.
  • If you lose the vault code, the copy on our relay is permanently undecryptable. Your local files are unaffected — they are plain Markdown on your disk — but we cannot recover the relay copy for you.
  • Pro is attached to a vault id, not to a person. Subscribing for a vault id you do not control simply pays for that vault.
